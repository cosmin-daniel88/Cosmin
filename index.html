FAQs

What are NFTs?

NFTs (non-fungible tokens) are unique digital objects stored on the blockchain. They can be art, music, code, memes, memories - anything that can be owned and verified. No copies. No fakes. Just you and the chain.

How do I get NFTs?

You can participate in drops, buy from the catalog, or whitelist your wallet.

How can we buy NFTs?

NFTs can be purchased by connecting your crypto wallet and completing transactions on the blockchain.
